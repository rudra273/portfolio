# Observatory assets

- `earth-day.jpg`: Earth surface texture from the Three.js examples, downloaded from https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg on 2026-11-07. This asset is used only for the new planet surface, not the original scene.
- Probe geometry, optical rings, solar panels and scene composition are newly authored procedural geometry in `src/components/observatory/ProbeScene.tsx`.
- The old scene files in `src/components/background/`, `PolishedSpaceBackground.tsx` and `StarField.tsx` are preserved. `/explore` mounts the historical scene separately.
